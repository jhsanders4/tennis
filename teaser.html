Format, repechage rules & day-by-day schedule

Format & scoring

  • Round of 22, Round of 12, Repechage & Quarterfinal: Fast4 — 4 games, one serve each, no-ad. At 2–2 a 7-point tiebreak decides the match.
  • #1 seed (Bo & Sarah) enter directly at the Round of 12.
  • Semifinal: best of 1 set, no-ad, tiebreak at 6–6.
  • Grand Final: best of 2 sets — match tiebreak decider if 1–1.

How the repechage works

  • After all 6 Round of 12 matches finish, every team (6 winners + 6 losers) is ranked by games won.
  • Ranks 1 & 2 get a bye straight to the Semifinal.
  • Ranks 3 & 4 go to the Quarterfinal.
  • Ranks 5–8 (2 winners + 2 best-placed losers) play the Repechage for the last 2 Quarterfinal spots.
  • Ranks 9–12 are eliminated.
